Travel Tips

Pack warm clothes, a rain jacket and sturdy shoes. Mountain weather changes quickly, and winters can be very cold.

Lesotho uses the loti (M). South African rand is also widely accepted. Carry cash when visiting rural areas.

A 4x4 may be needed for Sani Pass and some mountain roads. Check road conditions before travelling.
