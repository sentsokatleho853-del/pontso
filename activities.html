Activities

Discover exciting things to do in Lesotho.

Pony Trekking

Ride a Basotho pony along beautiful mountain trails.

Difficulty: Easy

Abseiling

Enjoy an exciting abseiling adventure at Maletsunyane Falls.

Difficulty: Extreme

Hiking

Explore mountains, rivers and villages on foot.

Difficulty: Medium

Skiing

Enjoy skiing and snowboarding at Afriski during winter.

Difficulty: Medium

Bird Watching

Look for beautiful birds, including the bearded vulture.

Difficulty: Easy

Culture Tours

Learn about Basotho music, traditional clothes and food.

Difficulty: Easy
