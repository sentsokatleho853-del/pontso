About Lesotho

Lesotho is known as the Kingdom in the Sky because it is a mountainous country located entirely above 1,000 metres. It is famous for its beautiful mountains, waterfalls, rivers and traditional villages.

Visitors can enjoy:

  • Beautiful mountain scenery
  • Pony trekking and hiking
  • Winter skiing
  • Traditional Basotho culture and food
